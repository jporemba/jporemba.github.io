PhD Student, Computer Science · Algorithms Lab, University of British Columbia

My name is Joseph. I am a PhD student in the UBC Computer Science department’s Algorithms Lab, where I am supervised by Bruce Shepherd. I obtained a Master’s degree in Computer Science at UBC, also supervised by Bruce, and a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science & Combinatorial Optimization at the University of Waterloo, where I wrote my undergraduate thesis under the supervision of Nancy Day.

My research is in network flow theory, and I broadly am interested in graph theory and discrete optimization. I also maintain an interest in formal methods and symmetry breaking.
